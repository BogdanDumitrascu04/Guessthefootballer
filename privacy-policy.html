Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Bogdy's Games is a free, non-commercial portfolio project.

What is collected

The site has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no ads. I do not collect any personal information.

What is saved in your browser

Your language and difficulty choices are saved in your browser's local storage, so the game remembers them on your next visit. They stay on your device, and clearing the site's data removes them.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Pages documentation.

Changes

If the site ever adds accounts or anything else that stores personal data, this page will be updated first.

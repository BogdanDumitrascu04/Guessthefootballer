Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Guess the Footballer is a free, non-commercial portfolio project run by Bogdan Dumitrașcu, in Romania. You can play every game without an account. An account is optional: it only keeps your rounds and your statistics.

Playing without an account

There are no cookies, no analytics and no ads. Your language and difficulty choices are saved in your browser's local storage, so the game remembers them on your next visit; they stay on your device. What you have played of the player of the day (the date, your guesses, your result) is kept there too, so a reload continues the round. Your browser also keeps a copy of the site's own files (the pages and the player lists), so the game opens without a connection and can be installed as an app; that copy contains nothing about you, and clearing the site's data removes it.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Pages documentation.

Every image, font and script comes from the site itself, so opening a page sends nothing to any other company. The one exception is the player of the day: its page asks the site's database, kept at Supabase, who the player is, so Supabase receives your IP address, as with any request. Without an account, nothing about you or your guesses is sent or stored there.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is handled by Supabase, which stores the account. From Google it receives your name, your email address, the link to your profile picture and your Google account ID. Your Google password is never shared. The site shows your name and email only to you, on your profile; the picture is not used.

For each round you start while signed in, the site keeps: the league, the season and the difficulty, when the round started and ended, the number of guesses and hints, whether you won, the points, and the round itself (the hidden player and your guesses), so that your profile can show your history and statistics; for the player of the day, also which day it was. A round you start and leave unfinished is kept as lost.

To keep you signed in, your browser's local storage holds a sign-in token; signing out removes it. Supabase also keeps a sign-in log (time and IP address) to protect accounts.

Where the data is kept

On Supabase servers in London, United Kingdom. The European Union recognises the United Kingdom as offering an adequate level of data protection.

Why, and who can see it

The data is used only to give you the features you asked for when you signed in: saving your rounds and showing your profile. It is not sold, not shared and not used for advertising.

Only you can see your rounds and statistics; the database itself enforces this, not just the website. A result link that you choose to share contains that round (the players and your guesses), but not your name or email.

How long, and how to delete it

Your data is kept for as long as you have an account. On your profile page, Delete account removes your account and every round you played, immediately. A copy may remain for a short time in the hosting provider's automatic backups, until they are overwritten.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to its use. Your profile already shows everything kept about your rounds, and the delete button removes it all; for anything else, write to the address below. You can also complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP.

Changes

If the site starts keeping any other kind of data, this page will be updated first, and the date above will change.

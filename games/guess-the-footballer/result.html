Guess the footballer

How to Play

1. Select a league, a season and a difficulty.

2. Guess the footballer based on the clues provided.

Green ✓ = same as the hidden player · ↑ ↓ = the hidden player's age or number is higher / lower

Hints: the club unlocks after 2 guesses, the age after 4.

Points: a win at the 1st guess gives 60 on Easy, 120 on Medium, 240 on Hard; each extra guess or hint costs one step (10 / 20 / 40).

3. Enjoy the game!
